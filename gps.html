UNDERSTANDING GPS

What Is GPS?

A powerful technology that helps us find our position anywhere on or near Earth.

GPS stands for Global Positioning System.

It is a satellite-based navigation system that helps determine the location of a device anywhere on or near Earth.

Your smartphone can use GPS to determine your location and help applications such as maps and navigation find the best route.

GPS works by receiving signals from satellites orbiting Earth and calculating the distance between the satellites and your device.

THE WORKING PRINCIPLE

How Does GPS Work?

GPS uses signals transmitted by satellites to calculate the position of a receiver on Earth.

🛰️

1. Satellites

GPS satellites continuously send signals containing information about their position and the current time.

📱

2. Your Device

Your phone receives signals from multiple GPS satellites and measures the time taken for the signals to arrive.

📍

3. Location

The device uses the received signals to calculate its position on Earth.

THE SCIENCE BEHIND GPS

The Science: Trilateration

GPS uses mathematics and signal travel time to determine your exact position.

GPS mainly uses a method called trilateration to determine a receiver's position.

Your device measures how long signals take to travel from different satellites.

Since the signals travel at a known speed, the device can estimate its distance from each satellite.

By combining information from several satellites, the device can calculate its position.

A GPS receiver generally uses signals from four or more satellites to determine a precise position and account for timing errors in the receiver's clock.
