What is GPS?



GPS stands for Global Positioning System.

It is a satellite-based navigation system that helps determine the location of a device anywhere on or near Earth.

Your smartphone can use GPS to determine your location and help applications such as maps and navigation find the best route.


How Does GPS Work?

2. Your Device

The device uses the signals to calculate its position on Earth.

The Science: Trilateration



GPS mainly uses a method called trilateration to determine a receiver's position.

Your device measures how long signals take to travel from different satellites. Since the signals travel at a known speed, the device can estimate its distance from each satellite.

By combining information from several satellites, the device can calculate its position.

A GPS receiver generally uses signals from four or more satellites to determine a precise position and account for timing errors in the receiver's clock.

Where Do We Use GPS?

  • 🗺️ Navigation and digital maps
  • 🚗 Finding routes while driving
  • 📦 Tracking deliveries
  • ✈️ Aviation and transportation
  • 🚑 Emergency and rescue services
  • 🏃 Fitness and activity tracking


💡 Did You Know?

GPS satellites continuously broadcast signals that can be received by GPS receivers on Earth.

Your phone can use GPS along with other location technologies to help determine your position.
