01 · INTRODUCTION

What Is a Washing Machine?

An everyday appliance powered by science and technology

Making Laundry Easier

A washing machine is an electrical appliance that cleans clothes automatically using water, detergent and the movement of a drum or agitator.

It saves time and effort by doing much of the work that was once done by hand.

It combines electricity, mechanical movement and chemical action to remove dirt from clothes.

02 · WORKING

How Does a Washing Machine Work?

Five main stages help clean your clothes

💧
STEP 01

Filling

Water enters the machine and mixes with detergent to prepare for washing.

🫧
STEP 02

Washing

The drum moves the clothes back and forth. Detergent helps loosen dirt and stains.

🚰
STEP 03

Draining

The dirty water is pumped out of the machine through a drainage system.

🚿
STEP 04

Rinsing

Fresh water removes the remaining detergent and loosened dirt from the clothes.

🌀
STEP 05

Spinning

The drum rotates rapidly to remove much of the water from the clothes.

03 · SCIENCE

The Science Behind Washing Machines

Different scientific principles work together to clean clothes

Scientific Principle How It Helps
Mechanical Energy The motor rotates the drum, moving the clothes and helping loosen dirt.
Chemical Action Detergent helps lift dirt and grease away from the fabric.
Water Movement Water carries away loosened dirt and detergent during washing and rinsing.
Centrifugal Effect During the spin cycle, rapid rotation helps water move out through the holes in the drum.
Electrical Energy Electricity powers the motor, controls and water pump.
04 · COMPONENTS

Main Parts of a Washing Machine

Important components that make the machine work

🥁

Drum

Holds the clothes during washing and spinning.

⚙️

Motor

Provides the power needed to rotate the drum.

🚰

Water Pump

Removes used water from the machine.

💧

Inlet Valve

Controls the entry of water into the machine.

🧴

Detergent Tray

Holds detergent before it enters the wash.

🎛️

Control Panel

Allows the user to select a washing programme.

05 · TYPES

Types of Washing Machines

Different designs are used for washing clothes

⬆️

Top-Loading

Clothes are loaded through a door at the top. Some models use an agitator or impeller.

⭕

Front-Loading

Clothes are loaded through a front door. The drum tumbles the clothes during washing.

🔄

Semi-Automatic

Usually has separate washing and spinning tubs, and some steps require manual operation.

06 · FUN FACTS

Did You Know?

Some interesting facts about washing machines

💡

The wash cycle and spin cycle use different drum speeds.

🌀

The spin cycle removes much of the water, but it does not completely dry the clothes.

🫧

Using the correct amount of detergent helps clean clothes without leaving unnecessary residue.

🌱

Choosing an appropriate wash programme can help save water and energy.

07 · SUMMARY

Conclusion

A washing machine is a great example of science in everyday life. It combines electrical energy, mechanical movement, water and detergent to make cleaning clothes easier.

Science makes everyday tasks simpler! 💧
