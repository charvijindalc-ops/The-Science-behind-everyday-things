BEYOND EARTH

🌌 Amazing Space Facts

01
☀️

The Sun Is Enormous!

Around 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun by volume.

02
🪐

Saturn Can Float!

Saturn's average density is lower than water. In a sufficiently large ocean, it would float.

03
🌙

The Moon Is Moving Away

The Moon moves away from Earth by approximately 3.8 centimetres every year.

04
✨

Space Is Silent

Sound needs a medium to travel. Since space is mostly a vacuum, sound cannot travel through it.

Your Heart Never Rests

Your heart beats continuously throughout your life, pumping blood around your body.

06
🦴

206 Bones

A typical adult human skeleton contains 206 bones.

07
🧠

Your Brain Is Powerful

The human brain contains approximately 86 billion neurons.

08

Taste and Smell Work Together

Your sense of smell contributes greatly to the flavours you experience while eating.

THE LAWS OF NATURE

⚡ Interesting Physics Facts

09
💡

Light Travels Fast

Light travels through a vacuum at approximately 300,000 kilometres per second.

10
🧲

Magnets Have Two Poles

Every ordinary magnet has a north pole and a south pole.

11
🌈

White Light Has Many Colours

White light can be separated into different colours using a prism.

12
🔊

Sound Needs a Medium

Sound travels through solids, liquids and gases, but not through a perfect vacuum.

WONDERS OF NATURE

🌿 Fascinating Nature Facts

13
🐙

Octopuses Have Three Hearts

Two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps blood to the rest of the body.

14
🌱

Plants Make Their Own Food

Plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make food through photosynthesis.

15
🐝

Bees Communicate Through Dance

Honeybees perform special movements called waggle dances to communicate food locations.

16
🌳

Trees Release Oxygen

During photosynthesis, green plants release oxygen as a by-product.
